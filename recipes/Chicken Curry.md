# Chicken Curry

## Ingredients
- 2000g Chicken Thigh https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/727622/woolworths-rspca-approved-chicken-thigh-fillets-skinless-tray
- 14g Canola Oil https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/750736/woolworths-canola-oil
- 400g Crushed Tomatoes https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/230392/ardmona-crushed-vine-ripened-tomatoes

## Method
1. Cut chicken into pieces
2. Brown chicken in canola oil
3. Add curry paste and crushed tomatoes
4. Simmer until chicken is cooked through
