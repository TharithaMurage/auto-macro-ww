## Method
1. **Bloom the Gelatin:** Pour **960ml** of chicken stock into a container and sprinkle **56g** of gelatin powder over the surface. Whisk briefly and set aside to hydrate.
2. **Render the Pancetta:** Heat **56g** of olive oil in a large (8L+) stockpot over medium-high heat. Add **300g** of chopped pancetta and cook until crispy and the fat has rendered.
3. **Brown the Meat:** Add **450g beef**, **450g pork**, and **450g lamb**. Season with salt and pepper. Use a potato masher or whisk to break the meat into very fine pieces. Cook until the moisture evaporates and the meat begins to sizzle and brown.
4. **Sauté the Vegetables:** Add **500g onion**, **300g celery**, **150g carrots**, and **40g minced garlic**. Cook for 15–20 minutes until the vegetables are soft and translucent.
5. **Add Aromatics:** Stir in chopped sage, parsley, and **60g tomato paste**. Cook for 1 minute until the paste darkens slightly.
6. **Deglaze with Wine:** Pour in **480ml white wine**. Scrape the bottom of the pot to release the fond. Simmer until the wine has evaporated and the pot is nearly dry.
7. **Combine Liquids:** Pour in **490ml milk** and the hydrated chicken stock/gelatin mixture.
8. **Slow Simmer:** Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Simmer uncovered for 1.5 to 2 hours, stirring occasionally, until the sauce is thick, rich, and the fat has emulsified.
9. **Cook Pasta:** Boil **1kg Pappardelle** in salted water until al dente. Reserve 2 cups of the starchy pasta water.
10. **Emulsify:** Add the cooked pasta to the sauce (or vice versa in a large vessel) with a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously over low heat for 1 minute to marry the sauce to the pasta.
11. **Finish:** Turn off the heat. Stir in fresh basil, parsley, grated Parmesan cheese, and a final drizzle of olive oil.
