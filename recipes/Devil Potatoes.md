# Devil Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 300g White Potatoes https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/208895/potato-white-washed
- 28g Canola Oil https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/750736/woolworths-canola-oil

## Method
1. Peel and cube potatoes
2. Fry in canola oil until golden
3. Add devil seasoning and toss
