# Pork Black Curry

## Ingredients
- 1500g Pork Shoulder https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/764541/woolworths-pork-shoulder-roast-boneless-boneless-small
- 42g Canola Oil https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/750736/woolworths-canola-oil

## Method
1. Cut pork shoulder into cubes
2. Brown pork in canola oil
3. Add curry paste and simmer until tender
