# Bean Curry

## Ingredients
- 250g Frozen Beans https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/768558/woolworths-frozen-sliced-beans
- 70g Canola Oil https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/750736/woolworths-canola-oil

## Method
1. Cook beans in canola oil
2. Add curry spices
3. Simmer until tender
