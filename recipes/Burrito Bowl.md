# Burrito Bowl

## Ingredients
- 400g Black Beans https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/663581/woolworths-black-beans-no-added-salt-can
- 210g Mozzarella Cheese https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/334934/devondale-shredded-mozzarella-cheese
- 463g Basmati Rice https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/694550/woolworths-basmati-rice
- 1000g Chicken Thigh https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/727622/woolworths-rspca-approved-chicken-thigh-fillets-skinless-tray
- 40g Fajita Seasoning https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/248679/old-el-paso-fajita-spice-mix-mexican-style
- 200g Sriracha Sauce https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/655419/huy-fong-sriracha-hot-chilli-sauce

## Method
1. Cook rice according to packet instructions
2. Season chicken with fajita seasoning and cook until done
3. Heat black beans
4. Assemble bowls with rice, chicken, beans, cheese
5. Top with sriracha to taste
