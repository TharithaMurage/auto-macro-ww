# Dahl Curry

## Ingredients
- 300g Red Lentils https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/8088/mckenzie-s-lentils-red

## Method
1. Rinse lentils
2. Cook with water and spices until soft
3. Season to taste
