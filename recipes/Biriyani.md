# Biriyani

## Ingredients
- 2000g Chicken Thigh https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/727622/woolworths-rspca-approved-chicken-thigh-fillets-skinless-tray
- 740g Basmati Rice https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/694550/woolworths-basmati-rice
- 100g Biryani Seasoning https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/437364/shan-seasoning-mix-biryani
- 28g Peanut Oil https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/73073/woolworths-peanut-oil
- 500g Greek Yogurt https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/463666/chobani-greek-yogurt-natural-whole-milk
- 350g Brown Onion https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/144329/onion-brown
- 250g Frozen Carrots https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/768562/woolworths-julienne-carrots
- 250g Frozen Beans https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/768558/woolworths-frozen-sliced-beans
- 40g Crushed Tomatoes https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/230392/ardmona-crushed-vine-ripened-tomatoes

## Method
1. Marinate chicken in yogurt and half the biryani seasoning for at least 1 hour
2. Cook onions in peanut oil until golden
3. Add chicken and cook until browned
4. Add vegetables and remaining seasoning
5. Layer with partially cooked rice
6. Cover and cook on low until rice is done
