# Burrito Bowl N

## Ingredients
- 800g Black Beans https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/663581/woolworths-black-beans-no-added-salt-can
- 370g Basmati Rice https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/694550/woolworths-basmati-rice
- 1500g Chicken Thigh https://www.woolworths.com.au/shop/productdetails/727622/woolworths-rspca-approved-chicken-thigh-fillets-skinless-tray

## Method
1. Cook rice according to packet instructions
2. Season and cook chicken until done
3. Heat black beans
4. Assemble bowls with rice, chicken, and beans
